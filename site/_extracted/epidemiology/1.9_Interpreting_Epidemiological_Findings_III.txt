Section 1 of 3: Confounding in epidemiological 
studies
What is confounding?
•
Confounding is the effect of an extraneous variable that wholly or 
partially accounts for the apparent effect of the study exposure, 
or that masks an underlying true association
•
Confounding leads to biased estimates and produces 
misleading results
