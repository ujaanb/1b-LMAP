What is personalised care?
•
A model of health and care that is centred around people having 
choice and control over the way their care is planned and 
delivered
•
It is based on ‘what matters’ to patients and their individual 
strengths and needs. 
•
Social Prescribing, Health & Wellbeing Coaches and Care 
Coordinators within primary care networks (PCNs) are core 
components of the Personalised Care model
Inclusion Health and the Lived Experience Project
What is inclusion health?
•
Inclusion health is a term used to describe any population group 
that is socially excluded
•
This can include those who experience homelessness, people 
with substance and alcohol dependence, vulnerable migrants, 
people who sell sex, victims of modern slavery, people in contact 
with the criminal justice system and people from Gypsy, Roma 
and Traveller communities, but can also include other socially 
excluded groups
Describe the impacts of being in an inclusion health group.
•
Inclusion health groups experience a significantly more severe 
burden of morbidity and mortality than the general public
•
Individuals within these groups often have multiple and complex 
needs with various clinical and social challenges
•
 As healthcare practitioners, you will work with patients 
from socially excluded groups throughout your career. Hence, it is 
important that you have an awareness of the health needs, beliefs 
and outcomes of these vulnerable groups.
