How many sustainable development goals are there?

17.

Recall the four humanitarian principles of global health.

  • Humanity
  • Neutrality
  • Independence
  • Impartiality

What is humanity?

Human suffering must be addressed wherever it is found, with particular attention to the most vulnerable.

What is neutrality?

Humanitarian aid must not favour any side in an armed conflict or other dispute.

What is independence?

Autonomy of humanitarian objectives from political, economic, military or other objectives.

What is impartiality?

Humanitarian aid must be provided solely on the basis of need, without discrimination.

Impartiality vs neutrality.

Impartiality means being fair and unbiased, while neutrality means being disengaged from a dispute or ideology.
