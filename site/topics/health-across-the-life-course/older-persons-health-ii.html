Profile of elderly health

Describe what the profile of elderly health in the UK looks like.

  • Around 90% of 80–84-year-olds do not have dementia.
  • UK census 2021: around 80% of people aged 90+ are not living in care homes; less than 4% of those aged 80–84 are in care homes.
  • 25% of people aged 100+ report good or very good health; around 30% were non-disabled.
  • NOTE: Many people still get to the end of their lives with limited or no restrictions from health.
  • However, absolute numbers of people living with dementia and other multiple long-term conditions are rising and represent a growing healthcare burden.
